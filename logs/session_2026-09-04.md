## Interpretation

Battery failure is now the leading hypothesis, but it is **not yet confirmed**. Other plausible causes include:

- deeply discharged or failed 2S battery pack;
- battery protection/BMS state;
- loose/disconnected battery connector;
- charge-path failure;
- power-button / power-path fault.

Period specification for this unit is 7.4 V 2500 mAh (2S nominal) battery.

Do not attempt cell-level recovery or bypass protection on an old lithium pack. If the pack is swollen, hot, mechanically damaged or smells abnormal, disconnect/use no further and replace/dispose of it through an appropriate battery route.
