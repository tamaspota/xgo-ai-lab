# Session 2026-09-05

## Objective

Diagnose why the original XGO-Mini no longer starts from its battery and inspect the exposed lower/controller board before deciding on firmware or hardware replacement.

## Battery findings

The chassis was opened. The robot uses two removable 18650-format Li-ion cells in an internal holder rather than an externally welded pack.

Historical documentation specifies 7.4 V 2500 mAh, so a 2S arrangement is expected, but electrical series wiring still needs direct holder-output measurement.

The two original cells were removed and placed in an XTAR VC2 charger. At the time photographed, the charger was not showing meaningful accumulated charge. Cell health is not yet known because individual cell voltages have not been measured directly.

Current next measurements:

- cell 1 open-circuit voltage;
- cell 2 open-circuit voltage;
- holder output with two known-good matched cells;
- holder/output voltage sag during power-on attempt.

Do not force-charge severely over-discharged cells or bypass protection.

## Lower/controller board inspection

User-read silkscreen includes `XGO MINI V2.5` and a 2021-era code/date marking. Exact full code remains to be captured more clearly.

Visible interfaces/features:

- lower-board micro-USB;
- 4-pin `SWITCH` connector for power-switch harness;
- 4-pin header labelled `G CLK DIO 3V3` (P2);
- board mode switches identified as DOWNLOAD and CALIBRATE;
- additional internal 4-pin connectors not yet mapped.

Engineering interpretation:

`G CLK DIO 3V3` is highly consistent with STM32 SWD: GND, SWCLK, SWDIO and 3.3 V reference. If electrically confirmed, the board can likely be inspected/recovered with an ST-Link-class debugger without replacing it.

The presence of micro-USB and DOWNLOAD/CALIBRATE controls on the lower board weakens the earlier assumption that PC COM3 must belong to the K210 AI module. COM3 could instead be a lower-board service/programming interface. Physical routing must be verified after stable power is restored.

## Architecture conclusion for now

Do not replace the lower board or display yet.

If the STM32 motion board and servo bus are healthy, the lowest-complexity modernization path is to retain:

- aluminum chassis;
- 12 original servos;
- original STM32 motion controller;

and replace/bypass only the K210 high-level layer later with a modern SBC/PC controller, camera and display if required.

Full motion-board replacement is justified only if the original STM32 board is faulty or its servo protocol proves impractical.

## Next concrete action

Measure the two original 18650 cells directly with a multimeter and report both voltages. If the cells are bad, use a matched known-good pair for a controlled power-on test before any firmware or board replacement work.
